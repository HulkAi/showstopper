About Me

I'm Alex Doe, a creative designer and developer with a strong mix of visual design, coding, and AI-assisted production skills. My work sits between UI/UX design, web development, branding, digital layouts, and experimental creative tools.

I enjoy building polished, professional visual systems — from websites, book covers, posters, logos, business cards, social media kits, and brand identities, through to interactive HTML/CSS/JavaScript layouts and editable SVG-based designs. I'm especially interested in creating work that is not only visually strong, but also practical, editable, responsive, and ready to use.

On the design side, I work with strong layouts, typography, colour palettes, visual hierarchy, image composition, SVG/pixel elements, and modern UI styling. On the coding side, I build with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and other tools to create interactive interfaces, automation scripts, and experimental design systems.

My focus is on fast, high-quality creative production: generating ideas quickly, refining them into polished designs, and building systems that make editing, presenting, and exporting work easier.
